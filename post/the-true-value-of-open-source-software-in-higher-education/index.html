The True Value of Open Source Software in Higher Education

Mar 10, 2019

1 min read

When an institution chooses open source software, they choose to be part of an active community of innovators. Open source software such as the Sakai LMS comes with a community that shares their experiences, ideas and customizations to make the product better for everyone.

New York University utilized the entire Sakai community to help deliver an improved LMS experience that raised their faculty satisfaction rates well beyond the national average.
